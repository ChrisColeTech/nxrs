### Cleanup and failure behavior

The future run consumes `attempt/` before moving shared STOP. It never touches the HARNESS3 latch. A refused
generic guard still consumes that receipt and reinstates shared STOP; it does not authorize another try.
The exact original bytes remain in `attempt/history/shared-before.json`. New shared bytes, including malformed
ones, are retained separately before merging their faults after old history. Existing `.pending` bytes are never
removed or rewritten; the driver also leaves a restoration journal after an otherwise clean run. The merged STOP
ends with `dc02-attempt-awaiting-review`, status/result or raised error. No path clears the MKW fault history.

The callback cancels scenario waits/pipes, joins its monitor, and lets the generic guard reap its process before
the existing SaveTransaction restores saves. Save failures, source changes, monitor errors and surviving claims
raise, while the outer receipt gate still restores STOP. There are no automatic link/game retries. Evidence I/O
failure retains the consumed attempt and any pending journal; do not delete those to force a later launch.
